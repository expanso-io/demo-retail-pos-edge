# Design brief

**Palette: light.** The subject is a high street: storefronts with striped
awnings on warm paper, recorded next to Expanso Cloud's light console. A
dark toggle is remembered per browser.

**One hue, one meaning.** Violet is Expanso (nodes, Cloud, control lanes).
Blue is a raw swipe with its card number. Teal is a shareable record and the
warehouse it lands in. Red is a record kept in the store. Amber is a dropped
network, a growing disk queue or a silent sensor. Nothing else is coloured.

**Motion is data.** Every particle is one measured event: a swipe a till
sent, a record the warehouse stored, a record a store kept, a record waiting
on disk, a sales window the display received. A till's face changes with its
state (idle, ringing up, closed, crashed, alarmed when its record is kept).

**Type.** Bricolage Grotesque (vendored) for words; monospace only for data
and identifiers.

**Archetype:** fleet into a governed boundary. Topology board (layout A):
stores inside the card business, the DMZ line, the warehouse; a shelf with
the before/after inspector and the quarantine.
